What is Joint Genesis?

Key Ingredients in Joint Genesis

According to the official Supplement Facts label, each capsule contains:

  • Hyaluronic acid matrix (60-75% Hyaluronic acid, >10% Polysaccharides, >5% Collagen Type I) – 80 mg
  • Pine bark extract (Pinus pinaster) – 25 mg
  • Boswellia extract (Boswellia serrata, gum resin, standardized to 65% Boswellic acid) – 100 mg
  • Ginger powder (root) – 200 mg
  • Black pepper (fruits, standardized to 95% Piperine, 50:1 extract) – 5 mg

The marketing materials refer to the hyaluronic acid matrix as Mobilee® and the black pepper extract as BioPerine®, though these brand names don't appear on the Supplement Facts label itself.
